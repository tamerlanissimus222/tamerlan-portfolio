title: "Distally Packaged Bionic Hand"
status: "Prototype"
description:
  - >-
    The range of bionic prostheses available for people with partial hand amputations or congenital limb differences at the metacarpal level, where the wrist remains intact and functional, is still limited. One of the main design challenges is finding space for the electronics and batteries, which are often housed in a forearm-mounted section. This adds bulk to the prosthesis and can affect both its appearance and comfort.
  - >-
    In these experimental projects, I explored whether the main electronics and batteries could fit into the space between the residual hand and the bionic fingers, which have their own built-in motors. I deliberately kept all these components within the natural outline of the hand, using two 18350 batteries. This was a design choice rather than a strict requirement. Allowing slightly more space would make it possible to use larger batteries or arrange the components differently.
  - >-
    EMG sensors would still need to be placed on the forearm in many cases. However, a forearm section designed mainly to hold these sensors could be much smaller and less restrictive than one that also houses the batteries and electronics.
  - >-
    The prototypes demonstrate that this approach is technically feasible and show its potential for developing more compact partial-hand prostheses that preserve wrist mobility and maintain more natural hand proportions.
